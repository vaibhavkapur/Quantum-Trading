Extrapolated cost at a target error

Evaluations needed come from the fitted convergence laws above. Classical time uses the measured NumPy cost per path. Quantum columns are assumption-driven estimates: sequential two-qubit layers at 1e-07 s (physical, no error correction) or 0.0001 s (logical), and 50 T gates per rotation. They ignore discretisation bias, routing, readout, repetition overhead and the error-correction footprint. Without error correction, a circuit with N CX gates only returns signal if the two-qubit error rate is well below 1/N.

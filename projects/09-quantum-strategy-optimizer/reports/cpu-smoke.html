Interpretation and constraints

Each selected strategy receives 1/K capital. Unused capital stays in cash. Sleeves are charged independently without cross-strategy trade netting. QUBO means and covariances use training returns only; validation chooses risk aversion; the test never chooses a configuration.

Grid visits feasible configurations; random samples them uniformly with replacement. TPE and annealing may evaluate penalized infeasible portfolios. QAOA uses finite measurements of a noiseless statevector; all measured candidates, repeats, and infeasible slack encodings consume budget. Equal evaluation caps are not equal computation or wall time. Grid stops early after exhausting its feasible domain. There is no classical repair or fallback in QAOA.

Objective gaps refer to the selected training objective, relative to exhaustive feasible search. MILP independently verifies each reference. Stability is adjacent-fold selection Jaccard similarity. Adjusted p-values use a one-sided HAC mean test and Bonferroni correction for the predeclared five-method search budget; normal approximations and finite samples limit inference. No deflated Sharpe claim is made.

The full resource CSV separates state preparation, compilation, simulation, sampling/decoding, scoring and optimizer overhead. Queue, communication, repair, and QPU cost are zero for these local simulations. No hardware benchmark or asymptotic speedup is implied.

Measurement notes

  • Separate training, probability calibration, threshold tuning and test windows. Label endpoints crossing a boundary are purged; embargo removes initial rows from later windows. PCA is fitted only on the shared training sample.
  • Means average folds within seed. 95% Student-t intervals describe seed variability on this one dataset; they do not quantify market uncertainty. A single seed has no interval. Incomplete groups must not be ranked as complete benchmarks.
  • p99 measures snapshot conversion, validation, feature generation, PCA, encoding, model inference, calibration and decision. Small samples make tail estimates unstable. Rows/s is serial in-process throughput, not network service capacity.
  • Net bps sums independent trade returns with one unit, observed bid/ask spreads, delayed fills, fees and adverse slippage. It is not a compounded portfolio return. Test trades do not overlap. Thresholds may select abstention.
  • Quantum runs use a noiseless local simulator. Finite-shot kernels sample exact overlaps with a binomial distribution and repair the training Gram matrix; VQC shots use PennyLane measurements. Cached simulator states cannot be assumed on hardware.
  • Trial time includes preprocessing, model initialization, training, calibration, threshold selection, test evaluation, streaming profiling and artifact writes. Shared process/data costs are shown separately. Native training calls can exceed the wall budget before being rejected.

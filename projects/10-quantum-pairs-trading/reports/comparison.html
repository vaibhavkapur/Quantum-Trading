Quantum-optimized pairs trading

Synthetic research · CPU simulation · no QPU claims

Train-only Engle–Granger screening, identical constrained selection objectives, validation-only entry tuning, and non-overlapping walk-forward tests. Prices, source hashes, budget settings, seeds and failures are recorded for reproducibility.

QAOA uses an exact noiseless expectation during optimization and finite shots for final samples. It is paired with disclosed classical feasibility repair. This experiment does not establish quantum advantage or live profitability.

Repeated invocations are deduplicated by comparison key, solver and seed, keeping the newest attempt. Different data, code or configurations are displayed in separate comparisons. Failed paths and explicit classical fallbacks are never silently pooled with successful quantum paths.

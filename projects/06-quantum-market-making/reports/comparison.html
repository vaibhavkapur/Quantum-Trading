Measurement protocol

Intervals are two-sided Student-t 95% confidence intervals across independent predeclared seeds. Repeated executions of the same seed are not independent samples; the newest trial is used. Small samples and correlated stress scenarios limit inference. Sharpe in CSV is a non-annualized within-window P&L-change statistic.

Quote p99 is the mean across runs of each run’s p99 Python-to-C++ local quote-and-ack duration, excluding optimizer barriers. It is not exchange latency. Raw trajectories include native and bridge timings. Full trial time includes calibration, validation, test replay, and optimizer waits; manifests additionally record loading, native build/cache, initialization, and full experiment time. Optimizer logs separate objective preparation, state preparation, compilation, iterations, sampling, decoding and communication.

QAOA uses a noiseless Qiskit statevector, exact expectation values for training and finite-shot final sampling. Every policy cost is enumerated classically before encoding. Exhaustive grid search is therefore a particularly strong baseline; no speedup or quantum advantage is claimed. No QPU, queue fee or network exchange is involved.

Only synthetic flow is bundled. FIFO queue placement from L2 snapshots is an assumption. Maker rebates and positive adverse-selection losses are separate; markouts at the end of a window are censored. Residual inventory after depth-limited liquidation remains marked to market. Backtests do not establish live profitability.

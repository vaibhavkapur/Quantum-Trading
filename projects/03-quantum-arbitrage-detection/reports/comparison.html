Metric definitions

  • Planted recall: share of planted profitable cycles detected at least once while active.
  • Search recall: detections divided by what exhaustive search finds on the same observed books. Isolates the search algorithm from data staleness.
  • Search recall without repair: the same, counting only cycles read from samples that already satisfied every QUBO constraint (equals search recall for non-QUBO solvers).
  • Recall vs true books: detections divided by cycles executable on the true (undelayed) books; includes misses caused by delayed or filtered quotes.
  • Nominal candidates / rejected by cost gate: negative-weight cycles a solver emitted, and how many failed liquidity-aware sizing against the minimum profit.
  • False positives after costs: detections that passed the cost gate on observed books but were not executable on the true books.
  • Missed-opportunity rate: share of ground-truth opportunity episodes with no profitable simulated execution.
  • Objective evals: candidate solutions scored. Cycles for enumeration and random search; edge relaxations divided by the maximum cycle length for Bellman–Ford and the bounded DP; reads × sweeps for simulated annealing; circuit executions (optimizer shots plus final shots) for QAOA.
